BRIDGE

Learning Platform

From reading to speaking and writing.

BRIDGE is a six-stage method. Students build vocabulary, retrieve evidence from a text, connect ideas, speak in the Bunker role-play discussion, write their own response and revise it.

BRIDGE — 6 Stages

The product of each stage becomes a resource for the next task.

B

Brainstorm

Recall relevant words and initial ideas before any support is given.

R

Read & Retrieve

Find the main idea and select one relevant detail from the text.

I

Ideas Connection

What does the text say? Why does it matter? What proposal follows?

D

Debate + Bunker

Role-play discussion — the speaking technique of the method.

G

Generate

Turn an idea refined in discussion into an independent written text.

E

Evaluate

Check against criteria, revise one sentence or justify it.

What is the BRIDGE Method?

BRIDGE is an author-designed method. Its goal is to move a learner from knowing words to saying and writing their own ideas.

STEP 01
FACT
"The text states that…"
→
STEP 02
MEANING
"This matters because…"
→
STEP 03
ACTION
"Our next step could be…"

This is the chain that connects reading to speaking and writing.

Bunker — the Speaking Technique

Bunker is an author-designed role-play discussion technique used at Stage D of the BRIDGE method.

Psychological ease

Learners speak not in their own name but as a role. Speaking anxiety is reduced.

Everyone speaks

Each role must present its opinion. No learner stays silent.

Text evidence

Learners are required to rely on evidence from the text in their spoken response.
